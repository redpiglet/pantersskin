# A checked playthrough - spoilers for the scheduling puzzle

Use this after a first attempt, or to check that the rules are being applied consistently. The PDF contains the complete playable rules; this walkthrough is optional.

Choose **Avtandil** as the armor carrier when the chambers open. Every row is one round with up to two sequential actions. Retrieve action markers and advance the courier at each round end.

| Round | First action | Second action | Courier at round end |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Avtandil sends his notice. Put N in Gulansharo. | No other character has a legal action yet. | N reaches At sea. |
| 2 | Avtandil travels to Sea Landing. | No other character has a legal action yet. | N reaches Pridon; set N aside and put the notice under Pridon's card. |
| 3 | Avtandil travels to Shore Road. | Pridon reads the notice; mark preparation 1. | Delivered. |
| 4 | Avtandil travels to the Reeds. | Tariel receives Nestan's letter and veil fringe; mark Letter received. | Delivered. |
| 5 | Avtandil travels to the Cave. | Tariel travels to the Cave. | Delivered. |
| 6 | Tariel opens the chambers; mark Chambers opened and give the armor bundle to Avtandil. | Avtandil travels to Riding Road. | Delivered. |
| 7 | Avtandil travels to Mulghazanzar. | Tariel travels to Riding Road. | Delivered. |
| 8 | Tariel travels to Mulghazanzar. | Asmat travels to Riding Road. | Delivered. |
| 9 | Asmat travels to Mulghazanzar. | Pridon gathers 300 horsemen; mark preparation 2. | Delivered. |
| 10 | Pridon readies the crossing; mark preparation 3. | Avtandil gives Pridon his armor. | Delivered. |

All three travelers have arrived, Pridon is ready, and the armor has been delivered. Finish immediately in **round 10** and read the final scene. The three knights' departure is automatic. Asmat stays in Pridon's household.

## Where a choice affects the timing

In round 9, giving Pridon the armor instead of gathering his horsemen is legal. However, Pridon then needs round 10 to gather the horsemen and round 11 to ready the crossing: he cannot take two actions in one round. The same reunion therefore takes **11 rounds**. Preparing him earlier also works; the schedule above is one example.

The solver also finds 10-round solutions with **Tariel** or **Asmat** carrying the armor. Carrier choice does not create an impossible game.

## What this verifies

Every completion needs eighteen character actions: six Avtandil travel actions, three Tariel travel actions, two Asmat travel actions, dispatching the notice, receiving Nestan's letter, opening the chambers, three Pridon preparation actions, and delivering the armor. Only Avtandil can act during each of the first two rounds. Those rounds use two actions, leaving sixteen actions and at least eight more rounds. Ten rounds is therefore a lower bound, and the legal schedule above reaches it.

This checks the rule economy and the target. It does not replace a human playtest. The first-play target of twelve rounds and the estimated duration are provisional.
